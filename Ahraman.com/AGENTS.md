# Creative tool settings

Use the shared `style.css` workspace styles for all creative tools. Artwork fills
the available page by default, with a retractable settings pane overlaying it on
the left, or at the bottom on phones. Opening or closing settings must not
resize or regenerate the artwork. Preserve a tool's artwork aspect ratio when
stretching would distort its meaning or output.

Start settings collapsed by default. Keep the smaller toggle left-aligned on
phones; when open, place the close arrow and title together at the top, followed
by the All patterns link. Order controls as page, shape and size, algorithm and
structure, visual settings, then seed, animation and actions. Keep tool-specific
controls independent and open the panel when needed to show an error.

Creative tools should provide page-size choices (current page, A4, A5, Full HD,
and 4K), a random seed where applicable, a shareable settings link, and a
black-and-white print-ready PNG. A4 and A5 PNGs use 300 DPI.
