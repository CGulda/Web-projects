# Creative tool settings

Use the shared `style.css` workspace styles for all creative tools. Artwork fills
the available page by default, with a retractable settings pane on the left, or
below the artwork on phones. Preserve a tool's artwork aspect ratio when
stretching would distort its meaning or output.

Start settings collapsed by default. Keep the smaller toggle left-aligned on
phones; when open, place the close arrow and title together at the top and the
All patterns link at the bottom. Keep tool-specific controls independent and
open the panel when needed to show an error.

Creative tools should provide page-size choices (current page, A4, A5, Full HD,
and 4K), a random seed where applicable, a shareable settings link, and a
black-and-white print-ready PNG. A4 and A5 PNGs use 300 DPI.
